# Review checklist — developing-investment-theses

These are authored procedure checks, not an assurance opinion.

## Domain checks
- Thesis has falsifiable underwriting conditions.
- Mandate and capability constraints are explicit.
- Evidence dates and counterarguments accompany claims.

## Stop-and-investigate example
The proposed strategy requires cross-border licenses not established by the evidence. Mark licensing feasibility an unresolved gating condition.

## Evidence record
For each material claim retain source ID, page/clause, as-of date, actual/forecast flag, currency, legal entity, reviewer and uncertainty. Use independent financial/legal review where necessary; do not mark a topic clean merely because records were not supplied.
