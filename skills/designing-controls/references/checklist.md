# Domain checklist

## Common failure patterns

- Writing be careful as a control.
- Letting the maker be the independent checker.
- Claiming policy publication proves operating effectiveness.

## Review questions

- Each control has a measurable objective and an accountable owner.
- Evidence is sufficient to test the relevant period.
- An unavailable approval mechanism fails according to explicit policy.

## Worked boundary

One administrator can both alter limits and approve transfers. Identify the segregation gap and a testable compensating control.

This is authored engineering guidance, not a legal opinion, live observation or certification. Validate jurisdiction-specific, version-specific and environment-specific requirements from authoritative sources.
