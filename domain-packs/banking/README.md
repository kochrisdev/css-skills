# Banking Domain Pack

**Skills:** 8

- `designing-core-banking` — Design core banking customers, accounts, products, transactions, posting and lifecycle controls.
- `designing-digital-banking` — Design secure digital banking journeys, identity, entitlements, transactions and servicing.
- `designing-loan-origination` — Design loan origination from application through KYC, underwriting, approval and disbursement.
- `designing-loan-management` — Design loan servicing, schedules, accruals, payments, delinquency, restructuring and collections.
- `designing-deposits` — Design deposit products, lifecycle, interest, fees, limits, holds and statements.
- `designing-bank-integrations` — Design controlled banking integrations with channels, rails, partners and enterprise systems.
- `designing-bank-controls` — Design banking controls for duties, limits, approvals, reconciliation, monitoring and evidence.
- `designing-bank-digital-twins` — Design governed bank digital twins for monitoring, simulation, decisions and scenario testing.
