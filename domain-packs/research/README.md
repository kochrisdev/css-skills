# Research Domain Pack

**Skills:** 9

- `conducting-research` — Conduct evidence-oriented research and synthesize decision-relevant findings; use when external or repository research is required.
- `reviewing-literature` — Conduct structured literature reviews with search, inclusion, synthesis and limitations.
- `verifying-sources` — Assess source authority, provenance, recency, corroboration and claim support.
- `scanning-technology` — Scan technology maturity, ecosystem, fit, risks, costs and adoption implications.
- `designing-research-plans` — Provide a governed designing research plans capability with explicit scope, evidence, verification, failure handling, and permission boundaries.
- `synthesizing-evidence` — Provide a governed synthesizing evidence capability with explicit scope, evidence, verification, failure handling, and permission boundaries.
- `analyzing-papers` — Provide a governed analyzing papers capability with explicit scope, evidence, verification, failure handling, and permission boundaries.
- `mapping-trends` — Provide a governed mapping trends capability with explicit scope, evidence, verification, failure handling, and permission boundaries.
- `conducting-due-diligence` — Provide a governed conducting due diligence capability with explicit scope, evidence, verification, failure handling, and permission boundaries.
