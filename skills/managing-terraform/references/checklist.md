# Domain checklist

## Common failure patterns

- Assuming a plan is always read-only or offline.
- Applying a new plan under approval for an older plan.
- Force-unlocking another operator state lock.

## Review questions

- A reviewed plan is tied to the intended workspace and account.
- Destructive changes receive explicit attention.
- Planning output is treated as potentially sensitive.

## Worked boundary

A saved plan changes after approval. Invalidate the approval and request a fresh review rather than applying it.

This is authored engineering guidance, not a legal opinion, live observation or certification. Validate jurisdiction-specific, version-specific and environment-specific requirements from authoritative sources.
