# Review checklist — modeling-fund-economics

These are authored procedure checks, not an assurance opinion.

## Domain checks
- Waterfall terms are document-specific.
- Gross/net and investor/fund scopes are not mixed.
- Unfunded commitments are not a substitute denominator for paid-in capital.

## Stop-and-investigate example
A proposed report divides 60m total value by 100m committed capital and calls it TVPI. Correct the denominator to matched paid-in capital and flag scope assumptions.

## Evidence record
For each material claim retain source ID, page/clause, as-of date, actual/forecast flag, currency, legal entity, reviewer and uncertainty. Use independent financial/legal review where necessary; do not mark a topic clean merely because records were not supplied.
