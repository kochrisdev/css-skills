# Review checklist — planning-pe-value-creation

These are authored procedure checks, not an assurance opinion.

## Domain checks
- Benefits have baselines, owners, timing and costs.
- Continuity and downside safeguards precede aggressive optimization.
- No unapproved personnel or financial action is executed.

## Stop-and-investigate example
Two initiatives each claim the same 500k procurement savings. Reconcile their overlap before rolling benefits into the investment case.

## Evidence record
For each material claim retain source ID, page/clause, as-of date, actual/forecast flag, currency, legal entity, reviewer and uncertainty. Use independent financial/legal review where necessary; do not mark a topic clean merely because records were not supplied.
