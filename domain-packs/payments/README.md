# Payments Domain Pack

**Skills:** 11

- `designing-fx` — Design FX quote and conversion capabilities covering sources, spreads, quote expiry, rounding, exposure, booking, reconciliation, and disclosure.
- `designing-remittance` — Design end-to-end remittance across onboarding, funding, FX, routing, compliance, payout, ledgering, reconciliation, and exceptions.
- `designing-payment-routing` — Design payment routing across rails/providers using eligibility, cost, reliability, compliance, retries, idempotency, and fallback rules.
- `designing-reconciliation` — Design deterministic reconciliation across ledgers, providers, banks, settlement files, fees, FX, timing differences, and breaks.
- `designing-payment-ledgers` — Design payment ledgers for authorization, settlement, fees, refunds, reversals and provider positions.
- `designing-settlement` — Design settlement positions, cutoffs, netting, prefunding, instructions, exceptions and finality.
- `designing-payouts` — Design payout orchestration across banks, wallets and cash networks with retries and exceptions.
- `designing-payment-webhooks` — Design secure, idempotent payment webhook ingestion, retries, observability and reconciliation.
- `designing-payment-exceptions` — Design handling for stuck, duplicated, rejected, reversed, delayed or mismatched payments.
- `designing-corridors` — Design remittance corridors across regulation, partners, currency, liquidity, routing and settlement.
- `monitoring-payments` — Design payment monitoring across technical, fraud, compliance, settlement and customer signals.
