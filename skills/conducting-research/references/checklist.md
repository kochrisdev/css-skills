# Domain checklist

## Common failure patterns

- Inventing a citation that looks plausible.
- Presenting the publisher marketing claim as independent validation.
- Calling a partial search a systematic review.

## Review questions

- Every material external claim has a supporting source.
- Time-sensitive evidence has an as-of date.
- Conflicting evidence is represented rather than silently discarded.

## Worked boundary

No authoritative current source confirms a regulatory threshold. Mark it unverified; do not guess the threshold.

This is authored engineering guidance, not a legal opinion, live observation or certification. Validate jurisdiction-specific, version-specific and environment-specific requirements from authoritative sources.
