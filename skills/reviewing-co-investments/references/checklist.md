# Review checklist — reviewing-co-investments

These are authored procedure checks, not an assurance opinion.

## Domain checks
- Sponsor assertions are independently challenged.
- Fund, SPV and investor economics are distinguished.
- Conflicts and follow-on obligations are visible before any commitment.

## Stop-and-investigate example
The sponsor quotes gross asset returns as the LP net return while the SPV charges carry and expenses. Reconcile the layers and withhold a net-return claim.

## Evidence record
For each material claim retain source ID, page/clause, as-of date, actual/forecast flag, currency, legal entity, reviewer and uncertainty. Use independent financial/legal review where necessary; do not mark a topic clean merely because records were not supplied.
