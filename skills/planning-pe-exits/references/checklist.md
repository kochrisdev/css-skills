# Review checklist — planning-pe-exits

These are authored procedure checks, not an assurance opinion.

## Domain checks
- Valuation ranges are not treated as committed offers.
- Continuation and related-party conflicts are disclosed.
- Net proceeds and timing include conditional or retained exposure.

## Stop-and-investigate example
The only price evidence is an old internal mark, and sponsor-affiliated buyers propose a continuation deal. Require current evidence and independent conflict review.

## Evidence record
For each material claim retain source ID, page/clause, as-of date, actual/forecast flag, currency, legal entity, reviewer and uncertainty. Use independent financial/legal review where necessary; do not mark a topic clean merely because records were not supplied.
