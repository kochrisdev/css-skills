# Domain checklist

## Common failure patterns

- Copying an AML rule from one country into all corridors.
- Inventing a monetary threshold.
- Treating a vendor checklist as regulatory authority.

## Review questions

- Each obligation has a source section and applicability context.
- Effective dates and stale evidence are visible.
- Unsupported legal conclusions are not converted into requirements.

## Worked boundary

An old circular and a new regulation conflict. Preserve both effective dates and refer the conflict for qualified review.

This is authored engineering guidance, not a legal opinion, live observation or certification. Validate jurisdiction-specific, version-specific and environment-specific requirements from authoritative sources.
