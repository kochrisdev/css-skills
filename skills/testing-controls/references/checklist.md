# Domain checklist

## Common failure patterns

- Testing only examples selected by the control owner.
- Extrapolating a tiny convenience sample to all operations.
- Deleting exceptions after remediation without preserving history.

## Review questions

- The sample can be traced to a defined population.
- Each conclusion cites sufficient evidence.
- A missing approval record is not silently assumed to exist.

## Worked boundary

The population completeness cannot be established. Report a scope limitation rather than certifying operating effectiveness.

This is authored engineering guidance, not a legal opinion, live observation or certification. Validate jurisdiction-specific, version-specific and environment-specific requirements from authoritative sources.
