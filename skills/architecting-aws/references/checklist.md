# Domain checklist

## Common failure patterns

- Using administrator access as a default architecture.
- Ignoring data egress and observability costs.
- Equating infrastructure diagrams with a security review.

## Review questions

- All cost and capacity estimates expose assumptions.
- Trust and account boundaries are explicit.
- Recovery claims include a proposed restoration test.

## Worked boundary

The deployment region is not approved. Keep the design conditional and do not provision resources.

This is authored engineering guidance, not a legal opinion, live observation or certification. Validate jurisdiction-specific, version-specific and environment-specific requirements from authoritative sources.
