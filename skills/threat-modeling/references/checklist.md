# Domain checklist

## Common failure patterns

- Listing generic vulnerabilities with no system-specific path.
- Treating the absence of an exploit as proof of safety.
- Using the model as a penetration-test authorization.

## Review questions

- Each threat crosses or abuses an identified boundary.
- Each mitigation is testable and owned.
- The model distinguishes assumptions from observed controls.

## Worked boundary

A diagram omits third-party credential storage. Mark it as an evidence gap and assess the conditional risk rather than inventing storage details.

This is authored engineering guidance, not a legal opinion, live observation or certification. Validate jurisdiction-specific, version-specific and environment-specific requirements from authoritative sources.
