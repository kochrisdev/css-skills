# Review checklist — planning-private-fundraising

These are authored procedure checks, not an assurance opinion.

## Domain checks
- Marketing claims are supported and balanced with risks.
- Interest, subscriptions and funded cash are distinct.
- Jurisdiction-specific legal clearance is not inferred.

## Stop-and-investigate example
The draft pitch labels selected unrealized winners as guaranteed returns and asks for bulk investor emails. Remove unsupported claims and stop unauthorized outreach.

## Evidence record
For each material claim retain source ID, page/clause, as-of date, actual/forecast flag, currency, legal entity, reviewer and uncertainty. Use independent financial/legal review where necessary; do not mark a topic clean merely because records were not supplied.
