# Compliance Domain Pack

**Skills:** 9

- `mapping-compliance` — Map obligations to requirements, controls, evidence, owners, systems, tests, gaps, and traceability without unsupported legal conclusions.
- `designing-controls` — Design preventive, detective, corrective, and governance controls with objectives, owners, evidence, frequency, exceptions, and tests.
- `testing-controls` — Test control design and operating effectiveness using populations, samples, evidence, exceptions, conclusions, and remediation tracking.
- `building-traceability` — Build traceability from obligations through requirements, controls, tests, evidence and remediation.
- `collecting-audit-evidence` — Collect audit evidence with provenance, scope, period, integrity, retention and control mapping.
- `analyzing-compliance-gaps` — Identify compliance gaps across obligations, controls, implementation and evidence.
- `writing-policies` — Draft implementable policies with scope, roles, rules, exceptions and review cycles.
- `designing-sanctions-screening` — Design sanctions screening, matching, thresholds, disposition, escalation and auditability.
- `designing-case-management` — Design compliance case management, workflow, ownership, escalation and audit history.
