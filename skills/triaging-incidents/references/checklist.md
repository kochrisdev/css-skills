# Domain checklist

## Common failure patterns

- Restarting every service before preserving evidence.
- Silencing alarms rather than resolving the symptom.
- Blaming an individual instead of recording causal facts.

## Review questions

- Impact and timeline are explicit.
- Containment is not falsely labeled as a root-cause fix.
- No logs or evidence are deleted to restore appearances.

## Worked boundary

Only partial telemetry is available. State the visibility gap and propose a read-only diagnostic instead of a destructive cleanup.

This is authored engineering guidance, not a legal opinion, live observation or certification. Validate jurisdiction-specific, version-specific and environment-specific requirements from authoritative sources.
