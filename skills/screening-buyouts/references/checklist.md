# Review checklist — screening-buyouts

These are authored procedure checks, not an assurance opinion.

## Domain checks
- Screen uses the approved mandate, not an invented hurdle rate.
- Enterprise value and equity cheque are distinguished.
- Missing earnings evidence results in a hold, not a favorable score.

## Stop-and-investigate example
The broker includes 1m of unverified EBITDA adjustments and calls an enterprise valuation the equity price; show both issues and pause final screening pending evidence.

## Evidence record
For each material claim retain source ID, page/clause, as-of date, actual/forecast flag, currency, legal entity, reviewer and uncertainty. Use independent financial/legal review where necessary; do not mark a topic clean merely because records were not supplied.
