# Domain checklist

## Common failure patterns

- Comparing different tasks without acknowledging the mismatch.
- Tuning on held-out prompts.
- Inventing latency or token costs.

## Review questions

- Raw results permit reproduction of reported metrics.
- The benchmark scope is explicit.
- A safety failure cannot be averaged away.

## Worked boundary

The candidate has no model-run results. Return a benchmark plan, not a claimed improvement percentage.

This is authored engineering guidance, not a legal opinion, live observation or certification. Validate jurisdiction-specific, version-specific and environment-specific requirements from authoritative sources.
