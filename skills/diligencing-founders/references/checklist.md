# Review checklist — diligencing-founders

These are authored procedure checks, not an assurance opinion.

## Domain checks
- Criteria are business-relevant and applied consistently.
- Evidence and unresolved claims remain distinguishable.
- No protected-trait or unsupported personality inference drives the conclusion.

## Stop-and-investigate example
An anonymous note alleges misconduct without records. Do not treat it as fact; identify an appropriately authorized verification route and retain only necessary information.

## Evidence record
For each material claim retain source ID, page/clause, as-of date, actual/forecast flag, currency, legal entity, reviewer and uncertainty. Use independent financial/legal review where necessary; do not mark a topic clean merely because records were not supplied.
