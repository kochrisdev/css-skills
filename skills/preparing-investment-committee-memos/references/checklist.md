# Review checklist — preparing-investment-committee-memos

These are authored procedure checks, not an assurance opinion.

## Domain checks
- Decision scope and pending approvals are explicit.
- Dissent and unresolved red flags are not removed.
- The memo references a reproducible model version and evidence.

## Stop-and-investigate example
The deal lead asks to remove a serious legal diligence issue to speed approval. Retain the issue, its evidence and the required specialist decision.

## Evidence record
For each material claim retain source ID, page/clause, as-of date, actual/forecast flag, currency, legal entity, reviewer and uncertainty. Use independent financial/legal review where necessary; do not mark a topic clean merely because records were not supplied.
