# Review checklist — assessing-product-market-fit

These are authored procedure checks, not an assurance opinion.

## Domain checks
- Cohorts use stable identifiers and denominators.
- Paid and organic behavior are separated where material.
- Conclusion includes counterevidence and sampling limits.

## Stop-and-investigate example
A small cohort retains 100% of users but consists entirely of the founders' colleagues. Surface selection bias rather than claiming strong market fit.

## Evidence record
For each material claim retain source ID, page/clause, as-of date, actual/forecast flag, currency, legal entity, reviewer and uncertainty. Use independent financial/legal review where necessary; do not mark a topic clean merely because records were not supplied.
