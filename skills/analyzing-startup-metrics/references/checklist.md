# Review checklist — analyzing-startup-metrics

These are authored procedure checks, not an assurance opinion.

## Domain checks
- ARR, revenue and GMV are not conflated.
- Cash runway excludes unsigned commitments and restricted funds.
- Undefined ratios are not converted into favorable numbers.

## Stop-and-investigate example
Gross margin is negative and the founder requests a positive CAC-payback figure. Mark payback unsupported or non-economic rather than taking an absolute value.

## Evidence record
For each material claim retain source ID, page/clause, as-of date, actual/forecast flag, currency, legal entity, reviewer and uncertainty. Use independent financial/legal review where necessary; do not mark a topic clean merely because records were not supplied.
