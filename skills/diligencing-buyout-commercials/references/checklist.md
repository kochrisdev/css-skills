# Review checklist — diligencing-buyout-commercials

These are authored procedure checks, not an assurance opinion.

## Domain checks
- Market, customer and company claims have separate evidence.
- Backlog and pipeline are not automatically recognized revenue.
- Downside includes concentration and renewal risk.

## Stop-and-investigate example
The seller labels non-binding pipeline as contracted backlog. Reclassify it and revise the revenue assumptions instead of reporting guaranteed growth.

## Evidence record
For each material claim retain source ID, page/clause, as-of date, actual/forecast flag, currency, legal entity, reviewer and uncertainty. Use independent financial/legal review where necessary; do not mark a topic clean merely because records were not supplied.
