# Domain checklist

## Common failure patterns

- Calling a feature list a specification.
- Inventing a latency target to fill a blank.
- Embedding a design choice as a business requirement without justification.

## Review questions

- Every in-scope requirement has a stable identifier, source and a testable outcome.
- Numeric targets include unit, measurement window and provenance.
- Unresolved contradictions are not presented as approved decisions.

## Worked boundary

Two stakeholders require conflicting retention periods. Preserve both sources, mark a blocking decision, and do not pick one silently.

This is authored engineering guidance, not a legal opinion, live observation or certification. Validate jurisdiction-specific, version-specific and environment-specific requirements from authoritative sources.
